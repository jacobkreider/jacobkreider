Film Photography

Notes from learning to shoot film

I'm learning to shoot film on a Pentax 17, a half-frame 35mm camera. Each exposure uses half of a regular 35mm frame, so every shot comes out as a vertical 3:4 picture and a 36-exposure roll gives me about 72 photos. The film stock and month for each photo are in its caption.
