Story Seed

A ghost ship has been sighted! It is rumored to be full of treasure.

Important NPCs

  • Loki - Loki, the God of Mischief, shows up to trick us once again! He is out to steal the treasure, and disguises himself quite cunningly.

Adventure Highlights

  • We begin by sailing towards the Isle of Shimmering Diamonds so that Jet Candle Youbaba can have a well-earned vacation.
  • Once the ghost ship was spotted, Jet Candle Youbaba chose to delay her vacation and sail over to see if the rumors of treasure were true
  • She uses her tornado powers to capture bioluminescent jellyfish for the crew's dinner
  • She defeated a giant, ship-eating whale
  • A small mouse helped her find the treasure hidden aboard the ghost ship
  • But, the mouse turned out to be Loki!
  • Using her tornado power, she got the treasure back from Loki
  • She flew to the Isle of Shimmering Diamonds, towing the ship behind her
  • We were welcomed by the Welcome Narwhal and Jet Candle Youbaba finally got to begin her vacation!
