The Quest for the Israeli Crystal

Played on: 2025-02-05

Setting

The story takes place in the Hot Air Balloon Region of Elvendale, and takes our hero through the Cave of Lost Memory, the Ice Giants' Lair, and the Dark Crystal Display Room

Main Character

The Unbeaten Queen of Israel
The Unbeaten Queen of Israel travels with her friend and former pet, Walter the Kangaroo.

Story Seed

Walter has discovered the location of the mythical lost crystal, but cannot travel there alone.

Important NPCs

  • Walter the Kangaroo - Walter is a kangaroo that was made intelligent by magic. The Queen realized he was more human than pet, so she set him free and he now travels the world, seeking adventure.

Adventure Highlights

  • Upon meeting Walter and hearing of the crystal, the Queen takes a hot air balloon to the shores of the Turquoise Lake.
  • She cannot land on the far side of the lake, for the grass is like razors and would pop her hot air balloon. So, she and Walter take a rowboat.
  • On the journey across, the lake begins to glow with errie red lights under the surface. These are the fish, summoning a giant worm monster that rises from the water.
  • The Queen defeats the monster with a blow from her trident
  • The Queen must enter the Cave of Lost Memory, where a curse erases the memory of any who enter.
  • She generates a magical force field bubble that protects her from the curse. But, when they enter, she realizes she forgot to protect Walter! He loses all memory of himself and her adventures.
  • She manages to restore enough of his memory for him to remember himself and who she is, but he is still very confused and silly for the rest of the journey
  • Next, they enter the Lair of the Ice Monsters and are greeted by four giant, armored ice monsters
  • Fighting them one at a time, the Queen must combine her magic powers and expert fighting skills to defeat them. Each monster is stronger than the last, but she finally wins-- thanks to her trusty trident!
  • Next, she uses her magic to unlock the steel door guarding the jewel display room. Inside are 30 guards protecting the stolen jewels. Using her magic and fighting skills, she makes short work of the entire group, defeating them in only three fights!
  • There is a giant snake guarding the crystal, but she defeats it with her bomb and takes claim of the mythical crystal!
  • Creating a portal, she travels instantly to Israel and has the magical doctors heal Walter. She is welcomed by a grateful crowd
  • And, once again, Teddy wins Amazing Tales!
