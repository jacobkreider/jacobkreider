Jet Candle Youbaba

Created by Teddy on 2024-01-22

Description

Jet Candle's sister, Jet Candle Youbaba is a formidable warrior in her own right. Carrying a shield and spear, her buffalo-like horns can strike fear in enemies. Her friends, however, know where her loyalties lie by the large 'A' on the front of her shirt, a sure sign she is a member of Jet Candle's team. In addition to her gadget belt, she is covered in magical scars from her adventures.
