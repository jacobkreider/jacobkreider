The Unbeaten Queen of Israel

Description

The Unbeaten Queen of Israel was born in Asgard and traveled through a magic portal to become Queen of Israel. She has long, flowing hair and wears a crown that can function as a gun. Her weapons arsenal includes a sword, a trident (her most feared weapon), a bomb, and a pickaxe. She can fight multiple foes at once with her six arms. She has a distinctive scar on her bellow that has magical power.
