Stor

Description

Stor is the first character Teddy ever created. He wears a power suit and a magic hat in addition to his battle helmet. Stor carries lots of different weapons and a lightsaber for close quarters combat. He also has a magic potion that he can use to make wishes come true.

Ability to make lightning disappear ★★
Highly skilled at using his magic wand to cast spells ★
